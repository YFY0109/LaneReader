手法	Method
点一下换一种，三种手法没有绝对好坏：	Click to switch; none of the three is strictly better:
· AdoFalling 原版：只看相邻音的间隔，密集段整条手序就是楼梯，	- AdoFalling original: looks only at the gap to the next note; in dense passages the fingering is a staircase,
  处理不了双押（同时的两颗音只能挤在一起）。	  and it cannot handle double presses (two notes at once end up in one lane).
· 拆手序（移植自 ReADOFAIMacro，MIT）：基于节拍、依赖原曲 BPM，	- Hand split (ported from ReADOFAIMacro, MIT): beat based, needs the chart's own BPM,
  强拍给主手、弱拍给副手，手序最合理，双押和轮指都能处理；	  strong beats to the main hand and weak beats to the off hand - the most sensible fingering, and it handles both double presses and rolls;
  但三连音交互会很灾难——窗口按 180° 切，三条 1/3 拍的音只在	  but triplets are a disaster: windows are cut at 180 degrees, so three notes a third of a beat apart only form
  归一化缩放正好为 1 时才凑成一个窗口，否则边界落进组里，整段错位。	  one window when the normalising factor happens to be exactly 1 - otherwise the boundary lands inside the group and everything after it shifts.
· 时间重置法：和谱面 BPM 完全无关，只有「换手时间」一个概念，	- Time reset: nothing to do with the chart's BPM, one idea only (how long before the hand resets),
  各种奇怪节拍都能处理；但每次一定从主手开始，不看强弱拍、	  so any odd rhythm works; but it always starts on the main hand and ignores strong and weak beats,
  不考虑节奏，只考虑怎么让手指舒服。	  thinking only about what is comfortable for the fingers.
